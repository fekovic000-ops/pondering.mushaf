Pondering Mushaf - offline app

1. Unzip this folder on your computer.
2. Go to app.netlify.com/drop (free account recommended) and drag the whole folder in. You get a web address starting with https.
3. On your iPhone, open that address in Safari. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
4. Open the app once with internet so it saves itself. After that it works in airplane mode.
5. Before you switch, copy your old notes: open the old link, go to Backup, tap Create backup. In the new app, go to Backup, paste, and tap Restore.
